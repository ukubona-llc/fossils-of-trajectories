Agent, Manifold, Dynamics, Trace, Objective — and the K08 that was already there

A single roundtable exchange, archived verbatim. It begins with a correction to Demis Hassabis's three conditions for AI flourishing, reconstructs them as a five-term pentad grounded in dynamics rather than optimization, arrives at an epistemology in which data are not observations but fossils of trajectories, and closes on an unplanned continuity: the same structure sitting, unnoticed, in the Specific Aims page of a 2020–2024 K08.

Companion volume: ukubona-llc.github.io/monograph — Piecewise Differentiable Ontologies. This page is a sibling artifact, not a revision: append-only, verbatim, diff-verifiable against the source exchange.
I · The Reconstruction

Hassabis Begins at Optimization.
The Pentad Begins One Level Deeper.

Demis Hassabis's stated conditions for AI to flourish: a huge search space, a clear objective function, lots of data. The roundtable's first move was to ask what these correspond to physically — and to catch a transposition.

PhysicsHassabisWhy
SpaceSearch spaceThe manifold you're exploring.
AgentDataThe walkers, trajectories, experiences. Data are the traces left by agents through the manifold.
TimeObjectiveTime is what distinguishes how trajectories accumulate. The objective determines which temporal dynamics are rewarded.

Said differently: search defines where you can go. Data records where you've been. Objective determines how history changes the future. The objective isn't merely "maximize score" — it implicitly chooses a class of temporal dynamics. Chess rewards long coherent trajectories (ballistic). Go is already more diffusive — influence propagates over the board. Language is extremely diffusive — meaning emerges statistically across billions of trajectories. Population health is almost purely diffusive — no single patient determines policy.

So the objective isn't "score." It's what kind of propagation the system is optimizing: trajectory, or field. That makes Hassabis's second condition much deeper than it first appears — not a scalar to optimize, but the equation of motion. You don't just specify where particles may exist (the space); you must also specify the dynamics.

II · The Pentad

Agent. Manifold. Dynamics.
Trace. Objective.

The reconstruction rewrites Hassabis's three conditions as five prior ones — not extensions, but the conditions that make the other three meaningful in the first place. Before you can search, you need something that moves and somewhere for it to move.

  • Agent — something that moves.
  • Manifold — somewhere to move.
  • Dynamics — the laws governing trajectories (ballistic, diffusive, mixtures, constraints).
  • Trace — event streams recording those trajectories.
  • Objective — the criterion by which trajectories are evaluated or redirected.

Demis's three requirements then fall out naturally:

Massive combinatorial search space→the manifold
High-quality real / simulated data→traces
Clear objective function→objective

This is less an extension of Hassabis than a subsumption. His formulation begins at optimization. The pentad begins one level deeper, at dynamics — and once you start there, optimization becomes just one particular way of selecting among possible trajectories.

The Ukubona Mapping

Applied directly to digital twins, the pentad relabels as follows — with the objective moved deliberately to position V, because in biology it doesn't exist until you've decided what game you're playing:

PosAIUkubona
IAgentWalker (patient, clinician, molecule, enterprise)
IIManifoldState space / care space / social space
IIITrajectoryDiffusion or ballistic motion through that manifold
IVEvent streamTimestamped traces ("data") left behind
VObjectiveFitness / utility / survival / prediction / intervention

Evolution had no reward function written down. People simply wandered. Hospitals accumulated charts. Cells accumulated mutations. Companies accumulated transactions. The reward function is inferred afterwards. That is why Hassabis's formulation felt slightly "inside-out" for biological systems: AlphaGo begins with rules — an objective given in advance. Ukubona's digital twins begin with trajectories, because biology doesn't hand you the objective up front.

III · Dynamics as Memory

Ballistic Retains Direction.
Diffusion Destroys It.

There are only a handful of fundamental ways to move through a manifold — and they aren't primarily equations about particles. They're equations about memory.

Ballistic — Δx ∝ t — the walker remembers where it was going.
Diffusive — ⟨Δx²⟩ ∝ t — the walker forgets.

Ballistic motion retains directional information; diffusion destroys it. That is why stochastic gradient descent begins ballistic — large coherent gradients — and becomes diffusive near an optimum. That is why children learn ballistically and experts refine diffusively. That is why an epidemic initially spreads along travel networks before becoming community transmission. That is why tiki-taka alternates between purposeful attack and local diffusion until a passing lane suddenly appears.

The walkers, trajectories, experiences. Data are the traces left by agents through the manifold.

— the line that reopened the pentad

Ukubona digital twins live off event-streams: timestamped, attributed — the closest thing to ontology, and therefore to epistemology.

IV · An Epistemology

Knowledge Isn't Stored Objects.
It Is Reconstructed Trajectories.

The "data" are not the agents. They're not even the observations. They are the fossils of trajectories. That changes everything.

The ontology isn't Patient → Diagnosis → Medication. The ontology is Event, at time t, caused by agent x, in state y. Everything else is derived. That's exactly what event sourcing does in distributed systems. It's also what physics does. And — arguably — what history is.

Every timestamp is a tiny tangent vector. Every event stream is a sampled geodesic. A digital twin is simply the best estimate of the hidden path connecting those samples.

Reality consists of trajectories. Observation consists of fossils. Knowledge consists of reconstructing the trajectory from its fossil record. Prediction consists of extending that reconstructed trajectory into the future. Intervention consists of bending it.

Paleontologists don't observe dinosaurs; they observe traces from which trajectories and behaviors are inferred. Physicists don't observe electron paths directly; they reconstruct them from detector hits. Astronomers infer stellar evolution from photons that left millions of years ago. Ukubona's event streams are exactly that: fossil records of motion through a latent manifold.

V · The Unplanned Continuity

The Footprints of Missingness
Were Always Footprints of Trajectories.

Verbatim — the prompt that closed the loop

"Fossils of trajectories rekindles something from the specific aims page of my K08 (2020–2024): to trace the footprints of missingness — I expected to not find data on perioperative and long-term risks following nephrectomy in older living kidney donors... and here we are talking of fossils of trajectories!"

The K08 wasn't really about "missing data." It was about missing trajectories. The conventional view treats missingness as a matrix with empty cells — a static ontology. The K08's actual question was fundamentally temporal: what happened to this person after nephrectomy? The missingness wasn't a blank value. It was an unobserved continuation of a path through state space.

A creatinine measurement isn't "a datum." It's evidence that, at time ti, a kidney occupied a particular region of physiological state space. A hospitalization isn't "an outcome." It's a point on a path. Death isn't a binary endpoint. It's the terminal boundary condition of a trajectory.

This reframes causal inference itself. Traditional epidemiology asks: what is the treatment effect? The trajectory view asks: how does treatment bend the geodesic? The second question is geometrically richer — not estimating a coefficient, but estimating how interventions alter the shape of paths through a manifold.

Reality consists of trajectories. Observation consists of fossils. Knowledge consists of reconstructing the trajectory from its fossil record. Prediction consists of extending that reconstructed trajectory into the future. Intervention consists of bending it.

— compact epistemology, arrived at from K08 through Ukubona

The object of study never changed between the K08 and Ukubona LLC. Only the substrate did: sparse perioperative records in one case, continuous event streams in the other. Fifteen months and two decades apart, the same question was being asked — what is the shape of the path connecting these fossils? — long before either roundtable methodology or TMVES existed to name it.
